Common questions

How do I restore tint Pro?

Use the same Apple Account you used for the purchase, then open tint’s Settings or paywall and tap Restore Purchases. An internet connection is needed to check your purchase.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone or iPad, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions and select tint. Cancel there to stop future renewals. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Lifetime is a one-time purchase and does not renew.

What can I try for free?

You can edit a photo and complete your first successful export for free. After that, importing a new photo or exporting again requires tint Pro.

Why can’t I save to Photos?

Check tint’s Photos permission in your device’s Settings and make sure you have enough free storage. You can also use the app’s share option to save your export elsewhere.

Can I request a refund?

Apple handles App Store payments and refund requests. Visit Apple’s Report a Problem page to request a refund.

Does tint order physical prints?

No. tint creates digital images with a printed look. You can export your result as JPEG, PNG, or PDF and use a printing service of your choice.
