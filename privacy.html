Privacy policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

tint is developed by Muhammet Emin Kocaman. This page explains how the app and this website handle information.

Your photos

tint processes photos on your device. We do not upload your photos to our servers or to RevenueCat. The app accesses the photos you select and requests permission when saving to your photo library. When you share an export, the destination you choose handles that file under its own policies.

Local app data

Editing state, preferences, saved presets, and information about your free export are stored locally. A free-export marker is stored in the device’s Keychain and may remain after uninstalling the app.

Purchases and Pro access

Apple processes payments. tint uses RevenueCat to verify purchases, manage Pro access, and restore purchases. This involves purchase records, subscription status, a customer identifier, and app/device information needed to provide the service. The app can derive a pseudonymous identifier from an App Store transaction identifier; you do not need to create a tint account.

We do not receive your payment card details. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy and Apple’s privacy policy for their practices.

Support messages

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you send. We use these to respond and investigate the issue. Please avoid sending sensitive information that is not needed for support.

This website

This site does not include advertising trackers, analytics scripts, or tracking cookies. When hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub may process technical request information, such as your IP address, to operate and secure its hosting service. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Retention and your choices

You can manage photo permissions in your device’s Settings. Local app files can be removed by deleting the app; exported images remain wherever you saved them. Purchase records may be retained by Apple and RevenueCat to provide purchase restoration and meet their obligations. For questions about access to or deletion of information we hold, contact us. Any applicable privacy rights depend on your location.

Contact and changes

Email muhammetkocamann@gmail.com with privacy questions. If these practices change, we will update this page and its date.
